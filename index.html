What are the components of a salary calculator?

Calculating your take-home pay involves subtracting all statutory deductions from your total earnings. Use this standard formula to verify your payslip:

Net Salary = Gross Salary – Total Deductions

Total Deductions typically include contributions to EPF, SOCSO, EIS, and PCB (Monthly Tax Deduction).

Salary Terms Comparison Table

Quick reference guide to the most common statutory terms found on Malaysian payslips:

Term Description Impact on Net Pay
EPF (KWSP) Employee Provident Fund for retirement savings. Reduces current net pay; increases long-term savings.
SOCSO (PERKESO) Social security protection for workplace injuries. Small deduction for comprehensive insurance coverage.
EIS (SIP) Employment Insurance System for job loss protection. Minimal deduction to provide a safety net during unemployment.
PCB (MTD) Monthly Tax Deduction for income tax payments. Varies based on your specific annual income bracket.

Salary Payment Subject to EPF

EPF contributions are mandatory for almost all types of monetary payments from an employer to an employee.

Included Items:

  • Basic salary
  • Bonuses and incentives
  • Commissions
  • Allowances (unless specifically exempted)
  • Arrears of wages

Excluded Items:

  • Service charges
  • Overtime payments
  • Gratuity and retirement benefits
  • Travel allowances

Salary Payment Subject to SOCSO

SOCSO contributions are calculated based on the employee's monthly wages, which includes various forms of remuneration.

Included Items:

  • Basic salary
  • Overtime payments
  • Commissions and service charges
  • Paid leave (annual, sick, maternity)
  • Allowances (fixed or variable)

Excluded Items:

  • EPF contributions
  • Traveling allowances
  • Gratuity and retirement benefits
  • Annual bonus

Salary Payment Subject to EIS

The Employment Insurance System (EIS) follows the same definition of "wages" as SOCSO for contribution purposes.

Included Items:

  • Basic salary
  • Overtime payments
  • Commissions
  • Paid leave payments
  • Fixed allowances

Excluded Items:

  • EPF and SOCSO contributions
  • Gratuity payments
  • Annual bonus
  • Reimbursements

Deadline Submission for Employers

EPF (KWSP), SOCSO (PERKESO), and EIS contributions must be submitted on or before the 15th of the following month.

  • Late payments may result in penalties
  • Deadlines are subject to changes by the respective authorities

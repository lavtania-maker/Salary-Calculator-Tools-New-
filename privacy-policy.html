Privacy Policy

How SalaryCalculator.my collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: 27 April 2026

1. Overview

SalaryCalculator.my ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website www.salarycalculator.my. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our salary calculator and related tools, how we use that information, and your rights regarding your data.

By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

SalaryCalculator.my is a client-side tool. All salary calculations are performed entirely in your browser — we do not transmit or store the salary figures, EPF contributions, SOCSO amounts, or any personal financial data you enter into the calculator.

We may collect the following non-personal information automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring URL
  • Approximate geographic location (country/region level, via IP address)

3. How We Use Your Information

The automatically collected data listed above is used solely to:

  • Analyse website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve the performance and usability of our tools
  • Diagnose technical issues

We do not sell, trade, or rent this information to third parties.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to understand how visitors interact with our site. Google Analytics places cookies on your browser to collect anonymous usage statistics. These cookies do not identify you personally.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

You may also disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of our website.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — website usage statistics
  • Google Fonts — font delivery
  • Firebase / Firestore — backend services for authenticated features

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them directly.

6. Data Security

Since no personal financial data is transmitted to our servers, the risk of data breach for your salary information is minimal. For any data we do hold (e.g. anonymised analytics), we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

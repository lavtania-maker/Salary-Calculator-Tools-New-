Annual Leave (AL) in Malaysia: Entitlement, Rules & How to Calculate

Annual leave (AL) is one of the most important employee benefits in Malaysia. It gives employees paid time off to rest, travel, or attend to personal matters while continuing to receive their salary.

Under the Employment Act 1955, eligible employees are entitled to a minimum number of annual leave days based on their length of service. Understanding these rules helps employers stay compliant and allows employees to better plan their leave.

What Is Annual Leave (AL)?

Annual leave (AL) is paid leave that employees earn each year during their employment. Unlike sick leave or public holidays, annual leave is usually planned in advance and requires employer approval.

Annual leave is separate from:

  1. Medical leave (MC)
  2. Public holidays
  3. Maternity or paternity leave
  4. Unpaid leave

Many employers provide annual leave as part of their employee benefits package, with some offering more than the minimum required by law.

Annual Leave Entitlement in Malaysia

The Employment Act 1955 sets the minimum annual leave entitlement based on an employee's continuous years of service.


Years of Service

Minimum Annual Leave

Less than 2 years

8 days

2 to 5 years

12 days

More than 5 years

16 days

These are the minimum statutory entitlements. Employers may provide more annual leave through employment contracts or company policies.

How to Calculate Annual Leave

Annual leave is generally calculated based on an employee's length of service. Employees who join or leave the company during the year usually receive pro-rated annual leave.

Annual Leave Formula

Annual Leave Entitlement = Annual Leave Days × Months Worked ÷ 12

Example

An employee:

  1. Joins the company in July
  2. Is entitled to 12 days of annual leave per year
  3. Works for 6 months during the leave year

Calculation:

12 × 6 ÷ 12 = 6 days

The employee is entitled to 6 days of annual leave for that year.

What Is Pro-rated Annual Leave?

Pro-rated annual leave applies when an employee does not complete the full leave year, such as when they:

  1. Join the company mid-year.
  2. Resign before the end of the leave year.
  3. Complete only part of the leave entitlement period.

Employers typically calculate the leave earned based on the number of completed months of service.

Can Annual Leave Be Carried Forward?

Unused annual leave may be carried forward if allowed under the company's leave policy.

Under the Employment Act 1955, annual leave should generally be taken within 12 months after the end of the leave year. If it is not taken within the permitted period, the entitlement may lapse unless the employer's policy states otherwise.

Employers should clearly communicate carry-forward rules to avoid misunderstandings.

What Happens to Unused Annual Leave?

If an employee resigns or their employment ends, any earned but unused annual leave is generally paid based on the employee's ordinary rate of pay.

The final payment depends on the employee's accrued leave balance and the terms of the employment contract.

Tips for Employers

To manage annual leave effectively:

  1. Create a clear annual leave policy.
  2. Track employee leave balances regularly.
  3. Apply leave approval consistently across the organisation.
  4. Inform employees about carry-forward and unused leave rules.
  5. Use an Annual Leave Calculator Malaysia or HR software to simplify leave calculations and reduce manual errors.

FAQs

How many annual leave days am I entitled to in Malaysia?

Under the Employment Act 1955, the minimum entitlement is:

  1. 8 days if employed for less than 2 years.
  2. 12 days for 2 to 5 years of service.
  3. 16 days after more than 5 years of service.

Employers may provide additional leave as part of their company benefits.

How is pro-rated annual leave calculated?

Pro-rated annual leave is usually calculated using this formula:

Annual Leave Days × Months Worked ÷ 12

This applies to employees who join or leave the company during the leave year.

Can annual leave be carried forward?

Yes. Whether annual leave can be carried forward depends on the employer's leave policy. Generally, annual leave should be used within 12 months after the end of the leave year.

Can an employer reject an annual leave request?

Yes. Employers may reject or reschedule annual leave if there are valid operational or business reasons. Employees should follow the company's leave application process before taking leave.
